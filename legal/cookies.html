Cookies

Last updated: November 2026

Short answer: none

boltproof.com sets no cookies. It loads no third-party scripts — no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels — so there is nothing to ask consent for and no consent banner to accept. A draft of the formal cookie notice, aligned with UAE practice, is pending review by UAE legal counsel and will be published with the final legal text.

What about linked sites?

Links on the site point to WhatsApp and to the Google Business Profile page. If you follow one, the operator of that destination service handles its own data and notices; their policies apply from that point, and we have no role in them.

If we ever add anything

If the site ever adds analytics or a similar function, a visible notice will explain what it stores and why, before it is enabled. Until then, this page — no cookies — is accurate.
