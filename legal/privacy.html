Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

This page describes what boltproof.com does and does not do with your information. It is the pre-launch notice for the site, published ahead of the full legal text, which is pending review by UAE legal counsel. Until that review completes, treat this page as the operative statement of what the site does.

What the website collects

  • Nothing is stored. The lead panel on the front page submits no form to any server. Pressing “Continue on WhatsApp” opens your WhatsApp application with a pre-filled message — the details you typed travel directly to your phone, not to this website.
  • No analytics, no tracking scripts. The site loads no third-party code: no ad scripts, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no beacons.
  • Demonstrations are synthetic. Every answer, citation and document shown in the demonstrations is illustrative sample content, clearly labelled as such. No customer document is displayed on this website.

What happens when you contact us

When you open WhatsApp, continue a conversation, or email us, we hold what you send us in order to respond to your request. What we do with that correspondence will be stated in the full legal text pending counsel review; in brief — it is read by the person answering, it is not sold, and it is not used for advertising. You can ask us to delete correspondence at any time.

Your data stays where you keep it

The private-AI service described on this site is offered on a principle of minimal access: it reads only the locations you approve (SharePoint, OneDrive or Google Drive), answers with citations back to those documents, and can be deployed in the cloud you choose, in your own private cloud, or on your premises. A full description of access, encryption and data handling for each deployment option will be part of the service documentation issued with the full legal text.
